** Misc

Zed didn't launch with a Linux version. That's happened frequently enough with
=k8s lens=, =DockerDesktop=, =GithubDesktop=, etc... so when I learn that an
"enthusiastic developer-centric business/product" explicitly _does not_ support
Linux on the desktop, i just really stop listening to everything about it.

... Why? Because i'm not waiting around one week at time just *hoping* for that
shit to arrive when it's frustrating you wouldn't launch with linux support --
this "anima" isn't directed at zed, per se. it's just accumulated residue from
isolation where I can't even see the tools software developers use on other
desktops.

Like i have no idea what features exist and it's frustrating when i learn years
later that "oh now there was a linux version for 2 years already". It's like
quadruple jeopardy for trauma (getting hurt like four times in rapid succession
the instant i discover whatever it is). anyways, i've known for awhile

anyways, it looks like _maybe_ i can remotely pair program. It's funny how large
of an effect that could've had on my life over the past 5-10 years.

#+name: extractBindings
#+begin_src jq :in-file "assets/keymaps/default-linux.json"
[path(.. | select(objects and .bindings)) as $p
  | { ($p + ["bindings"] | join("."))
      : (getpath($p)."bindings"
        | to_entries | map(if (.value | (type == "array"))
                           then [.key, (.value | first)]
                           else [.key, .value]
                           # then (.key + (.value | first))
                           # else (.key + .value)
                           # then ({(.key): (.value | first)})
                           # else {(.key): .value}
                           end | join(" ")))
    }
] | add | add | sort | unique
# | [.key, .value] | join("\n")
#+end_src
